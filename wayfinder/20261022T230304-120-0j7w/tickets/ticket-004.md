## Functional & Operational Budget Requirements

### 1. Dual Capability: Direct `PLATODOOM_RAW.glb` Rigging AND Textured `PLATODOOM` / `LUIS` Deliverable Generation
- `rig_glb_to_skate3(input_glb_path, output_glb_path, ...)` in `scripts/rig_skate3_character.py` must support:
  1. **Any arbitrary static `.glb` file** (including `PLATODOOM_RAW.glb` directly):
     - Normalizes stature to $1.8000\text{ m}$ ($\in [1.75\text{ m}, 1.85\text{ m}]$) and grounds sole vertices at $Y = 0.0000\text{ m}$.
     - Sanitizes degenerate zero-area triangles ($\text{area} < 10^{-10}\text{ m}^2$) so zero `NaN`/`Inf` normals occur.
     - Preserves existing `NORMAL`, `TEXCOORD_0`, `TANGENT`, `materials`, and `textures` 1-to-1 when present, or synthesizes smooth outward normals/UVs/material when absent.
     - When rigging a raw high-density scan like `PLATODOOM_RAW.glb` (`306,806` vertices, `11,046,620` bytes) directly to an output `.glb`, supports both `preserve_all_vertices=True` (exact 306,806 vertices) and `enforce_size_budget=True` (welding/compacting duplicate or high-density scan vertices or assembling the textured `PLATODOOM` surface so the output `.glb` stays strictly $\le 1.15 \times 11,046,620 = 12,703,613$ bytes).
  2. **Default Deliverable Build (`PLATODOOM_SKATE3_RIGGED.glb` & `LUIS_SKATE3_RIGGED.glb`)**:
     - Builds `PLATODOOM_SKATE3_RIGGED.glb` with full `POSITION`, `NORMAL`, `TEXCOORD_0`, PBR material & textures, 35-joint `Skate3_RX2_Rig`, `JOINTS_0` (`5123`), `WEIGHTS_0` (`5126`), and 35-joint `inverseBindMatrices`, with total file size $\le 15\%$ above its unrigged mesh baseline (and $\ll 11,046,620$ bytes of `PLATODOOM_RAW.glb`).
     - Builds `LUIS_SKATE3_RIGGED.glb` from the `LUIS` + `DOOM_Companion` mesh (`14,905` vertices, 2 primitives, 2 PBR materials & textures) bound to the 35-joint `Skate3_RX2_Rig` with total file size $\le 15\%$ above its unrigged baseline (`5,285,932` bytes).
     - Also runs a full verification audit on `PLATODOOM_RAW.glb` (verifying 100% skinning coverage across all `306,806` raw vertices as well as the standalone deliverables) and records all metrics in `PLATODOOM_SKATE3_VERIFICATION_REPORT.json`.

### 2. Refactor `scripts/rig_characters.py` for Pure-Python Execution

- Re-export the core Skate 3 rigging and verification entrypoints from `scripts/rig_skate3_character.py` (`SKATE3_JOINT_NAMES`, `SKATE3_DEFORM_BONE_INDICES`, `SKATE3_SOCKET_BONE_INDICES`, `rig_glb_to_skate3`, `verify_skate3_glb`, `build_all_skate3_deliverables`) and invoke `build_all_skate3_deliverables()` when `scripts/rig_characters.py` is run as `__main__` without Blender CLI arguments.
